Gym anxiety is real, common, and completely reasonable — especially walking into a serious training
environment after years away. It is also very solvable.

## The three fears that keep people at the door

- **"Everyone will watch me."** In practice, people at the gym are absorbed in their own training. And with a
  coach beside you the whole session, there is never a moment of standing around looking lost.
- **"I don't know how anything works."** That is not a character flaw — equipment is genuinely unintuitive.
  One-on-one coaching means every machine and movement is explained before you touch it.
- **"I'll hurt myself or embarrass myself."** A coached session is built at your current ability, with form
  instruction on every exercise. Nothing is left to guessing.

## How one-on-one coaching removes each barrier

Connor meets every client at the entrance, guides the entire visit, and selects exercises — and appointment
times — that fit your comfort level. The session is planned before you arrive, so there is no wandering
between machines. Over a few weeks, the unfamiliar floor becomes a place where you know exactly what you are
doing.

## Anxiety fades with repetition, not courage

You do not need to feel brave to start. You need a first visit that goes well, then a second one. Confidence
is the result of the routine, not a requirement for it.
