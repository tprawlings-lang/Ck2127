Meal prep does not require a chef's skills or a free weekend. A repeatable 90-minute Sunday routine covers
most of the week's hardest food decisions.

## The 90-minute structure

1. **Plan (10 minutes).** Pick two proteins, two vegetables, one starch, and one breakfast you will happily
   repeat. Boring is fine — reliable beats novel.
2. **Shop from a fixed list (30 minutes).** Keep a standing grocery list organized by store section. Same list,
   small swaps, every week.
3. **Batch-cook (40 minutes).** Roast one sheet pan of vegetables and one protein while a pot of rice or
   potatoes cooks. Season simply.
4. **Portion and store (10 minutes).** Divide into containers while everything cools. Label anything that will
   not be eaten within a couple of days.

## Storage and reheating basics

Refrigerate cooked food promptly, keep it 3–4 days at most, and reheat until steaming hot throughout. When in
doubt, follow the food-safety guidance at [foodsafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts) —
general education here, not a substitute for it.

## Why this works when diets have not

Nothing about this routine is a diet. It simply removes the nightly "what's for dinner" decision — which is
where busy weeks go wrong. Structure, not willpower.
