The unknown is half of what makes starting hard. So here is exactly what a first session with Connor looks
like, start to finish.

## Before you arrive

You will already have had the free 15-minute call, completed a short readiness questionnaire, and confirmed
your gym membership (coaching fees and gym membership are separate. Both Connor and each in-person client
maintain the membership required by The Gym Phoenix).

## Step by step

1. **Connor meets you at the entrance.** You never walk in alone or search for a stranger on a gym floor.
2. **You talk first.** Goals, history, comfort level, and anything you are nervous about. Nothing is assumed.
3. **A gentle starting-point assessment.** Basic movements at your present ability. This is a starting point,
   not a test, and there is no score to fail.
4. **A manageable coached session.** You learn a few foundational exercises with clear instruction, at a pace
   your body accepts today.
5. **A written next step.** You leave knowing exactly what happens next: your plan, your schedule, and what to
   do on days Connor is not there.

## What will not happen

No shame, no yelling, no punishment workout, no surprise sales pitch, and no comparing you to anyone else.
Soreness the next day should feel like "I did something," not "I cannot move."

> General education only, not medical advice. Conditions and injuries that need clearance are referred to the
> right professional first.
