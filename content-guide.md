# Content and Brand Voice Guide

## Voice

Patient, direct, nonjudgmental. Write for adults 40–65 who feel out of shape,
restarting, or uncomfortable in gyms. They should feel: *I will not be judged.
I can start where I am. I can ask questions before paying.*

## Use / Avoid

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Start at your current level. | No excuses. |
| Build strength that supports daily life. | Get shredded. |
| No crash diets or punishment workouts. | Melt fat fast. |
| You will know what to do next. | We guarantee results. |
| Ask questions before you commit. | Limited time only, act now. |
| Adults 40+ are the main audience. | Anti-aging / reverse-aging claims. |
| General nutrition education and habits. | Medical nutrition treatment or prescribed diets. |
| Healthy Cooking Coaching. | We cook and deliver your diet meals. |

## Hard prohibitions (never publish)

- Fake testimonials, fabricated transformations, stock before/afters, invented
  credentials, AI-generated fake results.
- "NASM certified" wording before the credential is active and verified.
- Any implication Connor owns/manages/represents The Gym Phoenix.
- Any gym membership dollar amount without written gym approval.
- Medical diagnosis, disease treatment, or promised weight loss.
- Prepared meals, delivery, or catering claims (v1 is instruction only).
- Countdown timers, fake scarcity, shame, fear, or pressure tactics.

## Service naming

Exactly three services, always these names: **Personal Training**,
**Nutrition Coaching**, **Healthy Cooking Coaching**. "Diet coaching" may
appear only as a natural search phrase in supporting copy, never as a label.

## Required recurring statements

- Independence: "CK2717 Fitness operates independently from The Gym Phoenix."
- Membership: coaching fees and gym membership purchased separately.
- Results: "Individual results vary." near outcomes/testimonials.
- Cooking: "instruction, not meal delivery, catering, or prepared-food sales."

## Article standards (resources)

- One real client question per article, answered in plain language.
- Connor as named author **only after he reviews the final article** — until
  then bylines read "CK2717 Fitness (pending Connor's review)".
- Publish + updated dates on every article; registry in `src/content/articles.ts`.
- Primary or recognized sources for health/food-safety/credential claims.
- Separate general education from individual medical advice (blockquote note).
- Each article links: one service, one pricing path, one contact path.
- No keyword padding, no near-duplicate city/ZIP articles, no thin mass pages.

## Testimonials (when they exist)

Only after meaningful delivered service, in the client's own words, with a
stored written release. First name + last initial unless full ID approved.
Never edited to change meaning, never purchased, never promising the same
result. Before/after photos need specific written permission and accurate dates.
